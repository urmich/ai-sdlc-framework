# Support

This is personal/community-maintained third-party OSS by Michael Urinovsky,
not Microsoft-owned, sponsored or approved software. Support is best effort:
there is no guaranteed response time, SLA, enterprise entitlement or promise
that a corporate environment permits the package.

## Public questions and bugs

Read the [documentation](docs/README.md) and
[installation troubleshooting](docs/installation/README.md) first.
Search existing reports at
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/urmich/ai-sdlc-framework/issues).
If necessary, open an issue there with:

- Expected/observed behavior and a minimal reproducible example.
- Package/source version, OS/architecture and Node version.
- Exact safe command/selector and sanitized error/exit.
- Relevant failed/missing test cases and links to shareable reports.

Do not upload credentials, signed URLs, private source, personal paths or raw
session/runtime logs. Private conversations, work-item ledgers and approval
receipts are not required. For intermittent failures, include frequency and
whether a minimal isolated retry reproduced the original failure; keep that
failure distinct from later success. No separate discussion forum, private mailbox or paid
support service is represented as available.

Suspected vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public Issues.
Use [GitHub's private report form](https://github.com/urmich/ai-sdlc-framework/security/advisories/new)
(GitHub sign-in expected). The maintainer enabled private vulnerability reporting,
with API readback confirming it on 2026-10-20. That setting observation does not
claim a submitted report or completed end-to-end reporting walkthrough.
External network, corporate approval and package/OS policy problems require the
appropriate administrator; an alternate channel is not a policy bypass.
